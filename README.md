## `02 / SELECTED WORK`

These projects represent different problems I'm exploring through software.
Their implementation and completion status vary.

### 🚚 RIDA — Freight Marketplace

**Connecting businesses that need transportation with the people who provide it.**

RIDA is a Rwanda-first freight marketplace designed to connect businesses
and shippers with individual truck owners and logistics companies.

The product direction includes freight discovery, carrier matching,
booking coordination, and delivery confirmation.

**Engineering focus**
- Marketplace architecture and business workflows
- React-based frontend development
- Express APIs and MongoDB data modeling
- Authentication and shipment-related operations

**Technologies:** `React` `JavaScript` `Express` `MongoDB` `Mongoose` `JWT`

**Focus:** Building a practical foundation for freight discovery and coordination.

---

### 📚 ReadIt 2.0 — A Library Beyond the Shelf

**A library for discovering, accessing, and understanding books.**

ReadIt is evolving from a command-line library management project
toward a broader platform for physical and digital books.

An important part of the vision is supporting works written by people,
AI models, or human–AI collaborations, with transparent information
about authorship and provenance.

**Engineering focus**
- Book catalogs, editions, and physical copies
- Digital access and library circulation
- Members, borrowing, and returns
- Human, AI, and collaborative authorship
- Data modeling and incremental migration

**Planned stack:** `TypeScript` `Next.js` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL`

**Focus:** Growing an existing learning project into a more complete library system.

---

### 🗂️ Noote — Organizational Task Management

**Make work visible. Keep responsibilities clear.**

Noote is a task-management platform designed to help organizations
assign work, monitor progress, and maintain visibility into daily operations.

Its intended workflows include task assignments, notes, reminders,
visits, comments, and file-based proof of completed work.

**Engineering focus**
- Organization-oriented workflows
- Task assignment and progress tracking
- Backend APIs and data persistence
- Authentication and application reliability

**Technologies:** `JavaScript` `Node.js` `Web Development` `Database Systems`

**Live frontend:** [noote-lime.vercel.app](https://noote-lime.vercel.app)

**Focus:** Making day-to-day work easier to coordinate and follow.

---

### 📰 Living Story AI — Continuous News Intelligence

**Track developing stories continuously.**

Living Story AI (LSAI) is a web application for following news stories
over time instead of treating each article as an isolated event.

Users can create tracked stories, discover related reporting, store sources,
and revisit a story to look for potentially significant developments.

When configured and available, an AI provider can help analyze articles
for information that may represent a new development.

**Engineering focus**
- News research and source aggregation
- RSS and Google News integrations
- YouTube results when configured
- Story and source persistence
- AI-assisted article analysis

**Technologies:** `Python` `FastAPI` `Jinja2` `SQLAlchemy` `SQLite`

**Focus:** Exploring how software can help people follow evolving stories
and understand what has changed over time.

*AI-assisted analysis is intended to support research, not replace source verification.*

---

### ✨ SERA — Cinematic Brand Experiences

**Digital experiences designed to feel like campaigns, not conventional websites.**

SERA is an experimental premium brand experience focused on cinematic
visual storytelling, product presentation, and scroll-driven animation.

Rather than treating a landing page as a collection of static sections,
the project explores how motion, pacing, transitions, and product imagery
can work together to create a cohesive brand experience.

**Engineering focus**
- Scroll-driven animation
- Interactive product presentation
- Motion design and visual storytelling
- Component architecture and responsive interfaces

**Technologies:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `GSAP` `ScrollTrigger`

**Focus:** Exploring the intersection of frontend engineering, animation,
and digital brand storytelling.

## `04 / HOW I BUILD`

I prefer learning through implementation rather than stopping at tutorials.

My development process is iterative:

<div align="center">

| 01 | 02 | 03 | 04 |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 💡 | 🔎 | 🏗️ | 🧪 |
| **Identify** | **Investigate** | **Build** | **Test** |

| 05 | 06 | 07 | 08 |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 🐛 | 🔧 | 📈 | 🚀 |
| **Debug** | **Refine** | **Learn** | **Improve** |

</div>

I try to understand not only how to make something work, but also:

- Why the architecture makes sense.
- How data moves through the system.
- What happens when an operation fails.
- How to validate input and protect application boundaries.
- Where the current implementation falls short.
- What should be improved next, and why.

A working prototype is a beginning. Reliability, usability, and real-world
feedback are what help turn it into a better product.
